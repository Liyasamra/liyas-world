About Me

Hi, I'm Liya! 👋

In her own words — and a few notes from Mom and Dad.

My Story

I turn 9 on October 21! I'm in third grade, and this year feels like a big step — new teacher, new classroom, more responsibility. I love reading chapter books, learning new Amharic words, and hearing stories about Ethiopia. I'm working on being someone people can always trust to tell the truth, even when it's hard, and on being patient when things don't go my way right away.

My Strengths (so far!)

Liya is moving from the early "foundation" years into a season of deepening — more independent thinking, a clearer sense of her Ethiopian Orthodox faith and history, and more ownership of her own choices and friendships. This year's two focus virtues are truthfulness and patience.

Family, Faith & Heritage

Rooted in Faith and Ethiopia ✝️

Liya is growing up Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Christian, in a family that holds her Ethiopian heritage and her faith close together — not as separate things, but as one story. Her school reinforces shared Christian foundations like daily prayer, Scripture, and character formation, but the specific traditions of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church — the sacraments, the saints, Ge'ez, the fasting calendar, the Divine Liturgy — are taught deliberately at home and at church.

☦
🕯️

Faith at Home

Daily prayer, a Saturday formation time, and Sunday Divine Liturgy followed by a family conversation about what she heard and what was confusing.

📜

Ethiopian History

Learning the story of Aksum and Lalibela as real places and real history — distinguishing documented history from religious tradition, and leaving deeper theological questions to a priest or Sunday school teacher.

🗣️

Amharic & Culture

Building Amharic vocabulary a little at a time, and learning Ethiopian food, music, and customs as living traditions, not performances.
